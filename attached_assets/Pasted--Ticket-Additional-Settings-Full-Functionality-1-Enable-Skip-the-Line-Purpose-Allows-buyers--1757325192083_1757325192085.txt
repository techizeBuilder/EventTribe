🎟️ Ticket Additional Settings – Full Functionality
1. Enable Skip the Line

Purpose: Allows buyers of this ticket to bypass general entry lines at the venue.

Implementation:

Add a flag skip_the_line: true in ticket settings.

Generate a special QR code / badge that shows “Skip the Line”.

At the event gate, staff can scan and allow entry through a fast-track line.

2. Password Protect

Purpose: Restricts ticket purchase to people who know a password/code. Useful for private sales or VIPs.

Implementation:

Ticket has password_protected: true and a password_value.

On checkout, users must enter the password.

Validation: allow purchase only if password matches.

3. Enable Bundle

Purpose: Allows selling ticket bundles (e.g., “Buy 5 tickets for the price of 4”).

Implementation:

Define bundle rules (min_qty, bundle_price, free_tickets).

Example: Buy 5 Regular tickets → pay for 4.

On checkout, auto-apply the bundle pricing if quantity matches.

4. Enable Early Bird

Purpose: Offers discounted price for early buyers.

Implementation:

Ticket has early_bird: true with start_date, end_date, and discount_price.

Before the deadline, users pay discounted price.

After deadline, price reverts to normal.

Display countdown on frontend (“Early Bird ends in X days”).

5. Cover Ticket

Purpose: A ticket that includes entry + additional benefits (e.g., free drink, food coupon, merchandise).

Implementation:

Ticket has cover_ticket: true with benefits: [“1 Free Drink”, “Backstage Access”].

On ticket generation, show included perks.

Venue staff can scan and redeem benefits.

6. Enable Combo Tickets

Purpose: Sell multiple tickets as a package (e.g., VIP + Parking + Food Coupon).

Implementation:

Ticket has combo: true with combo_items: [ticket_id1, ticket_id2, …].

When user buys, system automatically issues all linked tickets.

Checkout shows full combo package.

7. Enable Waitlist

Purpose: If tickets are sold out, users can join a waitlist. If someone cancels, waitlisted users are notified.

Implementation:

Ticket has waitlist_enabled: true.

When sold out, show “Join Waitlist” instead of Buy button.

Store waitlist entries (user_id, event_id, ticket_id).

On ticket availability, send email/app notification to next person in waitlist.
